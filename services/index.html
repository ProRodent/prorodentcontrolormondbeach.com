How the services combine.

Most jobs involve two or three. The usual order is inspection first, then trapping, then sealing, then a return trip. Cleanup and repair go at the end, since fresh insulation is pointless if animals still have access.

Each stage is priced on its own line. Approve trapping and sealing now, and hold cleanup or repair until your budget is ready.

What we leave to others.

There is no stand-alone urgent page. An urgent call reaches the same desk and gets the earliest opening. Roof work, medical questions and structural fixes unrelated to rodents are other people's jobs, and we tell you when one arises.

Questions about services.

Can I order a single service on its own?

You can. Services work independently, and the technician may point out other items worth a closer look.

Do you deal with raccoons or squirrels?

These twelve pages cover mice and rats. Should we find some other animal, you will hear about it along with a suggestion of who to call.

What is usually first?

The inspection, since it names the animal and finds the entries. Sealing follows trapping so nothing is shut inside a wall.
