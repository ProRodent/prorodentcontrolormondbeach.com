Before you call: keep the dust down.

Use this list each time pellets, urine stains or torn nesting turn up inside.

  • Slow, gentle wiping keeps dust down. Fast scrubbing lifts it.
  • Throw open the doors and windows and leave the room alone for around thirty minutes before anybody enters.
  • Wear gloves throughout and wash up for a good while at the end.
  • Saturate the waste and the surface around it, then leave it alone before the first wipe.
