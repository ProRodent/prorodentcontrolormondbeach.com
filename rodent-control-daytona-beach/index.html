Before you call: cleaning waste without stirring dust.

Everything in this box restates guidance from the Centers for Disease Control. It applies to any household, booked visit or not.

  • Brooms, shop vacuums and leaf blowers all spread dust, so none of them belongs near rodent waste.
  • Prop the doors open, let a breeze run through and return after about half an hour.
  • Keep gloves on for the whole job, peel them off carefully and wash your hands afterward.
  • Wet everything thoroughly, give it a few minutes, then lift it with paper towels.
  • One knotted bag can hold towels and a carcass together, with a second bag if the plastic is thin.
  • Anyone who becomes ill after contact with rodent waste needs a clinician, because health questions fall outside pest work.
