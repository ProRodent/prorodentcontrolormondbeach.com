Before you call, guard your lungs first.

Short version of the public health advice, with the original pages linked after it.

  • Slow, gentle wiping keeps dust down. Fast scrubbing lifts it.
  • Think of the first thirty minutes as ventilation time with no one inside.
  • Count every stained surface as contaminated: gloves during, a handwash after, with no shortcuts.
  • Wet everything thoroughly, give it a few minutes, then lift it with paper towels.
  • One knotted bag can hold towels and a carcass together, with a second bag if the plastic is thin.
